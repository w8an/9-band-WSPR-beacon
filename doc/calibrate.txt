   Transmitter Calibration Procedure.
   05may2025


How to calibrate.

Hold the BAND CHANGE button while resetting device. The WSPR transmitter will go into calibration mode. Calibration announcement will show on the OLED display

The display will show a calibration frequency. On receiver, tune to that frequency. The transmitter will repeat sending 2 seconds of transmission on unbiased frequency and 1 second of biased transmission. 

Write down the zero beat frequency of the 2-second transmission.

Press the BAND CHANGE button to go to the next band. 
Again, mark down the zero beat frequency of the 2-second transmission.

Repeat for all 9 bands.

Press the PORTAL button on the back panel to launch the configuration web server. 
Go to the address shown on the display using computer browser. 
Click the INFO button on the configuration page, then click the param link.
(Alternately, go to the web address shown and add /param to the URL.)

Enter the collected frequencies of the 2-second transmissions.

Click the SAVE button. 

Reboot the WSPR transmitter and again hold the BAND CHANGE button to enter configuaration mode.

Cycle though the bands and ensure the 1-second transmission is at exactly the frequency shown on the display.

When calibration is complete, reboot. 

Unit is now ready for operation.
